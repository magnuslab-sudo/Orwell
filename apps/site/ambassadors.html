Ambassadors

We are looking for ambassadors

Do you have a community on Discord, on TeamSpeak, on Root, on your own server or in another app? Bring the admin and the community to Orwell, and test the app with us. We are not selling you anything. You test it, and the people who make the app listen to you.

What the community gets

  • Direct contact with the people who make Orwell

    A private channel between the community and the people who make the app. You write there what breaks, and we answer there. There is no customer support queue in between.

  • A channel of your own

    The community has its own channel on Orwell, with the features it needs. The features the community asks for go first on our list of things to build.

  • Your name on the site

    Your name, or the community's, goes in the tribute section, at the end of this page. Only the name you want to see written goes in.

  • A badge in the app

    A star next to the ambassador's name, in the member list, on the person's page and in messages. Only the Orwell team gives it, and nobody can give it to themselves.

What we ask

  1. Bring the community

    The whole community, or part of it. It is enough that they are people who talk every day.

  2. Use the app for real for one month

    Conversations, voice rooms, screen sharing, the board. Everyday use, and not a quick visit.

  3. Tell us what breaks and what is missing

    Without a filter. What annoys you is what we need to know first.

How to apply

Create an account, go to the Orwell channel and write "I want to be an ambassador" with the name of your community. We answer there.
